This is an amino-chain. It is just amino acids, linked one after another, like beads on a string. One amino acid alone does not do much. Link a few together, and you have a peptide. Keep linking more, and at some point, it becomes a protein. Same building blocks, every time. The only real difference is how many links there are, and the order they come in. A short chain: peptide. A long chain: protein. That is the whole idea. Follow for more peptide math.
