## EN (@buddypept)
Temperature is not the only thing that changes the clock. Light can too. A peptide is a chain of amino acids, and some of those links absorb light, especially UV. Tryptophan and tyrosine are two of them. When they absorb that energy the molecule can oxidize (Kerwin & Remmele, J Pharm Sci 2007). The chain looks the same in the vial, but part of it has changed. Not every peptide is equally sensitive: it depends on the sequence. Stay tuned to learn which chains are more sensitive, and which are less. That is why many peptide labels say protect from light and keep it in the outer box until use. The box is not decoration. It is part of the storage instructions. Light is a clock you cannot see.
