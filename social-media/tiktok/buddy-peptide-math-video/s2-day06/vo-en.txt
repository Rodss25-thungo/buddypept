Temperature is not the only thing that changes the clock. Light can too. A peptide is a chain of amino acids. Some of those links can absorb light, especially ultraviolet light. Tryptophan and tyrosine are two of them. When they absorb that energy, the molecule can oxidize. The chain looks the same in the vial, but part of it has changed. Not every peptide is equally sensitive. It depends on the sequence. Stay tuned to learn which chains are more sensitive, and which are less. That is why many peptide labels say protect from light. The box is not decoration. It is part of the storage instructions. Light is a clock you cannot see. Day six of ten. Follow so you don't miss day seven.
