Same amino-chain, same peptide. Different rules. You might expect one peptide to have one shelf life. It does not. The published rules belong to the exact product, not just the molecule. Look at three real labels. Product A says use immediately after mixing, and throw away what is left. Product B says throw the pen away twenty-eight days after first use. Product C says fifty-six days after first use. Different products, different formulations, different rules. It can even happen with the very same active molecule: two products can carry different storage times. The formulation decides: the water, the preservative, the container, the pH. So a number you hear about the amino-chain is not a rule for your vial. The published label for the exact product wins. Day nine of ten. Follow so you don't miss day ten.
