Same amino-chain, same peptide. Different rules. You might expect one peptide to have one shelf life. It does not. The published rules belong to the exact product, not just the molecule. Product A says use immediately after mixing (Glucagon for Injection label, DailyMed). Product B says discard the pen 28 days after first use (Forteo prescribing information, DailyMed). Product C says 56 days after first use (Ozempic prescribing information, DailyMed). Different products, different formulations, different rules. It can even happen with the very same active molecule: two products can carry different storage times. The formulation decides: the water, the preservative, the container, the pH.
