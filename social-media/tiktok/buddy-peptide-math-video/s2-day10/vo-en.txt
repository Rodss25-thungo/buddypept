Nine days. One question. How long does the amino-chain, a peptide, last after the water goes in? Here is the honest answer. There is no universal peptide clock. There are two clocks: one for microbiology, one for chemistry. And the chemistry clock changes with the molecule, the formulation, the temperature, the light, the container, the handling, and the time. Change one, and the clock changes. That is why a number from a friend, a forum, or a video is not a rule for your vial. The published stability data for the exact product wins.
