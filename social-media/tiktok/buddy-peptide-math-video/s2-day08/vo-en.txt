Two vials. Both look perfectly clear. Are they the same? Your eyes cannot tell you. Cloudiness, flakes, or particles you can see are a real warning sign. Pharmacopeia standards treat visible particles as a reason to reject a vial. But clear does not mean unchanged. Much of what happens to the amino-chain, the peptide, is invisible. Chemical changes like oxidation leave the liquid looking exactly the same. And some clumps are far too small to see. Labs need instruments to count them. So a clear vial is not a clean bill of health. Cloudy is a warning. Clear proves nothing. The label, the storage, and the time are what you can trust. Day eight of ten.
