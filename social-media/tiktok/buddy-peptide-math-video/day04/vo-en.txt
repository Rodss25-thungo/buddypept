Your syringe says one hundred units. Ever wondered what a unit actually is?
One unit on a U-100 syringe is one hundredth of a milliliter. That's it.
Ten units? Zero point one milliliter.
Here's the catch: units measure volume, not peptide.
How much peptide is in those units depends on your concentration.
