A U-100 scale shows one hundred units. What is a unit, actually? One unit on a U-100 scale is one hundredth of a milliliter. That's it. Ten units is zero point one milliliter. Here's the catch: units measure volume, not peptide. The amount of peptide depends on the concentration. At two milligrams per milliliter, zero point one milliliter holds zero point two milligrams.
