## EN (@buddypept)
Bacteriostatic water sounds like a shield for everything in the vial. It has one job. Per the Bacteriostatic Water for Injection, USP label (DailyMed), it is sterile water with 0.9% benzyl alcohol, a preservative that helps suppress bacterial growth. That is why it is labeled multi-dose. Sterile water for injection has no preservative and is single use. What bac water does not do: it does not stop the chemistry clock, it does not extend peptide life, and it is not complete protection. The label, temperature, and handling decide the rest.
